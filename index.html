The local workshop at Haruru Falls

Haruru Auto & Diesel runs out of the industrial estate on Te Kahu Street, a short drive from Paihia, Waitangi and Ōpua. Locals drop the car in on the way to work and pick it up on the way home.

You get a clear quote before work starts, a phone call if we find anything extra, and an invoice that matches what we said. That is the whole system.

The brands on our wall

The signs on the building are not decoration. Each one is a standard the workshop signs up to and gets measured against.

MTA Assured

We are a Motor Trade Association Assured workshop. That means qualified people, honest quotes and a nationwide code of conduct that backs you if anything is ever not right.

Auto Super Shoppes

Part of Auto Super Shoppes, New Zealand's network of premium independent workshops. Membership is earned on workmanship, and your service history travels with you nationwide.

Castrol Service Centre

Every service uses Castrol oils matched to your engine's exact spec, petrol or diesel. The right grade, not whatever drum is closest to the hoist.

Kumho Tyre stockist

Authorised Kumho Tyre dealer. Quality rubber for cars, utes and 4WDs at a fair price, fitted, balanced and aligned right here in the workshop.
